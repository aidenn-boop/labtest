`601c7a666c97a3115a8852bba2411123c4081123` by giddygiddyboy. The full upstream MIT
notice is in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES and the team MIT license is in LICENSE.

Dockerfile builds for linux/amd64 and runs in the organizer sandbox.
